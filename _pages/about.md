I'm a political scientist specialising in political behaviour, and public opinion, and public policy, currently Senior Research Fellow at More in Common.

I did my PhD at Oxford, where I trained in experimental methods, causal inference, survey science, and computational social science. I've also been a visiting fellow at Harvard, and have worked as a political analyst and researcher for the BBC and the United Nations.

In the past, I've also lectured at Jesus College and Hertford College, Oxford.

You can find examples of my projects in `_pages/repositories.md` and publications in `_pages/research.md`.
