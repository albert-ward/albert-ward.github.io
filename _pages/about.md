I'm a PhD candidate at the University of Oxford, specialising in public opinion and political behaviour in democracies. I also study opinions on climate change, and attitudes towards the EU. Occasionally I teach classes and lecture undergraduate politics at Oxford.

I'm interested in survey methodology, and quantitative methods more generally. You can find some examples of my work in `_pages/repositories.md`

In the past, I've been a Stipendiary Lecturer at Jesus College and Hertford College in Oxford. I've also been visiting fellow at Harvard University and have worked for the BBC as a political analyst.
